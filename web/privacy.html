Your data in ABPlot

Back to the editor

The browser editor stores the measured plot and preferences on this browser. Photo overlay recovery copies are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. Download a plot JSON or photo project to keep a portable backup. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted, and private browsing may not retain it.

Photos and recognition

Photo overlays and local text recognition process images on your device. They do not send photos to an online recognition service. Downloaded photo projects and overlaid images include your photo, so share them only with intended recipients.

Optional online recognition is disabled unless the site operator configures it. When enabled, the interface asks you to explicitly send a prepared image. The server forwards that image to Microsoft Azure Document Intelligence and returns recognized text. Review the provider’s data practices before using online recognition for sensitive material. Local recognition and manual entry remain available.

What the server handles

The app has no analytics or advertising code. The hosting provider may keep ordinary request logs, including network addresses. The optional online OCR service uses a temporary session cookie, short-lived access-attempt counters, and a daily usage count. It does not write uploaded images to its application storage. Contact the site operator for hosting and service-specific policies.

Remove your local copies

In Pool photo overlay, choose a saved recovery and Delete recovery copy. To clear all ABPlot local data, use your browser’s site-data controls. Download anything you want to keep first. Files you downloaded are managed separately on your device.

Measurements

Check entered A/B distances and the side of each measured point. OCR output must be reviewed. Photo projection aligns points on one plane; it does not determine depth or take measurements from the image.
